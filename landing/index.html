We can't read your vault. That's the point.

Everything is encrypted on your device before it's sent. Our servers only ever store scrambled data, including your item names and URLs. If our database leaked tomorrow, it would contain nothing anyone could use.

  1. 01

    Two secrets, not one.

    Your vault opens with your master password plus a Secret Key that only exists on your devices and your printed Emergency Kit. A stolen database is useless without both.

  2. 02

    Your password never leaves your device.

    Sign-in uses a protocol (OPAQUE) that proves you know your password without ever sending it, not even scrambled.

  3. 03

    Locks itself.

    The vault locks after 5 minutes idle and when you close the browser, and copied passwords are wiped from the clipboard after 30 seconds.

Argon2id key derivation · XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption with a separate key per item · OPAQUE sign-in · mandatory two-factor login · strict Content Security Policy with no third-party scripts

Because only you hold the keys, we can't reset your master password. Keep your Emergency Kit somewhere safe.

Questions founders ask

What happens if I forget my master password?

We can't reset it, because we never have it. Your Emergency Kit holds your Secret Key; the master password is the one thing only you know. Write it down and keep the kit somewhere safe.

What if I lose my laptop?

Your vault is encrypted and locks itself. Sign out the lost device from Settings, then sign in on a new one with your master password, Secret Key and 2FA code.

Why not just use Chrome's password manager?

Chrome is fine for everyday logins. It doesn't store 2FA secrets, recovery codes, API keys or SSH keys, it doesn't separate your projects, and it won't let you share one project with a contractor and take it back later.

Do I still need an authenticator app on my phone?

No, Arevei Vault generates the same codes. If you like, keep both while you switch over.

Can you see my data?

No. Item names, URLs, usernames and passwords are all encrypted before they leave your device. We store data we can't read.

Is there a mobile app?

The web app works in your phone's browser today. A native iPhone app with Face ID and AutoFill is on the roadmap.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export an encrypted backup any time, or a plain CSV after re-entering your master password.
